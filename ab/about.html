Our Journey

From Equine Passion to Premium Brands

Kavari Polo was born from a deep-rooted passion for equestrian culture and the discipline of polo. Founded in Jaipur in 2024 by Kavin Gaur, our brand merges tradition with modern design to create apparel that embodies the spirit of the sport.

What started as a personal love for the game evolved into a full-fledged lifestyle brand. Every stitch, every design, every fabric choice reflects our commitment to excellence, authenticity, and the polo way of life.

Our mission is simple: to create premium clothing that honors the heritage of polo while embracing contemporary style. Each piece tells a story of craftsmanship, performance, and timeless elegance.
